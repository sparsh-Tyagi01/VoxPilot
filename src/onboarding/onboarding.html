Privacy-first by design

VoxPilot runs entirely on your device. Here's what that means:

  • ✓ Your voice is never sent to any server
  • ✓ Page content stays on your device
  • ✓ No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry
  • ✓ Personal data encrypted with AES-256
  • ✓ Delete all data any time from settings

One-time model download required (up to 1.2 GB for the Standard profile). All subsequent processing is fully local.

Microphone access

VoxPilot needs your microphone to hear voice commands. Audio is processed locally.

🎤
What to expect Your browser will show a permission dialog. Click Allow to grant microphone access to VoxPilot.
